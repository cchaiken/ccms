We walk, pacing our strides in a natural rhythm. Our speaking voices rise and fall, shifting in pitch and dynamics as we talk with one another. And when a familiar song drifts through the grocery store or fills a concert hall, we can't help but hum along. Everyone is musical. 

And music is magical!
* The Newborn Rhythm: Research shows that infants can detect beat and pitch patterns within days of birth, long before they speak.
* Distinct Neural Wiring: Some people who lose language after a stroke can still sing and appreciate music, suggesting the two rely on different networks in the brain.
* A Natural Mood Boost: Music engages the brain's reward system, releasing dopamine and endorphins that can lift your spirits.
