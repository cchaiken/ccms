I adore this time of year! The music, the celebrations, the time with family, and a chance to catch your breath after a long year.

For many of us, though, the holidays also bring added pressure and stress. That's where your breath comes in. It's one of the simplest tools you have for easing tension. We've all heard the advice, "Just take a deep breath." Here's how to actually use your breath to find calm, feel grounded, and let go of stress during this joyful and sometimes overwhelming season. And once you've found your breath, you can put it to work in a way that's just plain fun: singing.

## Try a gentle breathing exercise for singing
Give yourself a few quiet minutes. Relax your shoulders, soften your jaw, and breathe in gently through your nose and out through your mouth. Notice how your chest expands as you inhale.

Now release the air on a soft, steady "sss," as if you were slowly letting the air out of a balloon. As you do, try to keep your chest gently expanded rather than collapsing. That sense of lift is what supports your voice when you sing. When you need more air, simply breathe in again.

Try breathing in for four counts and out for six. If that feels easy, try six counts in and eight out. Keep your shoulders relaxed, and spend about five minutes on the exercise before letting your breath return to normal.

## Take that breath into a song 

Choose one line from a song you know well. Find a starting note that feels comfortable in your own vocal range. Listen for where the melody rises and falls, and notice its rhythm. That's ear training: listening, recognizing, and matching!

Now add the words. Take a low belly breath before you begin, and let the words ride on a steady stream of air. Start with short phrases, and breathe wherever you would naturally pause when speaking.

## Make a little music together 

The next time a holiday song comes on at the grocery store, sing along with abandon! If you have a houseful of guests coming over, take a few easy breaths before you open the door. And if someone starts singing while you're clearing the table, join in. See what happens when everyone knows the chorus.

## Take a breath, sing a little, and enjoy the season.
