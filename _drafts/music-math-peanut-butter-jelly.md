---
title: Music and Math are like peanut butter and jelly...
date: 2026-04-01 00:00:00 Z
---

I remember being told that music and math were directly connected to each other and thinking, "yeah, right.", but, then I also remembered one of my parent’s friends mention that her family lived in the apartment building across from Albert Einstein, and that she recalled hearing him practice his violin. He was an accomplished musician! Once I started thinking about it, the examples kept coming: Pythagoras discovered the mathematical relationships between musical notes, satirist Tom Lehrer was a mathematician, and Art Garfunkel earned a master's degree in math.
Recently, one of my students invited me to take part in her Girls' Group Extracurricular Science Project. She was going to test many volunteers and see what their vocal ranges were. She had seen a similar test online and was going to research it for herself. She had some of her classmates and parents sing the lowest to highest notes that they could comfortably sing. She documented each volunteer’s result and came to the following conclusions: 
Her conclusions were:
1. Professional singers have a larger vocal range than untrained singers.
2. Every singer has a different vocal range.
3. People had fun singing and taking part in the study.
4. Girls love science!
It’s a thrill to know that your child is making lifelong connections on their own. Music and math are like peanut butter and jelly! They’re  good on their own and even better when they’re together!
